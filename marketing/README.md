## 🎯 Target Audience Snapshot

| Segment | Profile | Primary Driver | Barrier to Adoption |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Traditional Nanay/Tatay Store Owners** (Ages 40-65) | Operates sari-sari store out of front window; relies on spiral notebooks (*cuaderno*). | Stopping debt loss (*utang leakage*), saving time on mental math. | Fear of complicated technology; fear of losing data; low storage phones. |
| **Next-Gen Micro-Entrepreneurs** (Ages 20-39) | Tech-savvy, active on GCash, Facebook Marketplace, and TikTok. | Maximizing net margin (*tubo*), inventory efficiency, multi-payment tracking. | Annoyance with monthly SaaS subscriptions or apps requiring continuous Wi-Fi. |
| **Relief Family Cashiers & Helpers** | Teens, adult children, or in-laws minding the store part-time. | Avoiding checkout mistakes, remembering prices, not miscalculating change. | Complex menus or confusing cashier screens. |

## 💎 Core Value Propositions (The 5 Pillars)

1. **100% Offline Autonomy (Walang Data, Walang Load, Tuloy ang Negosyo)**:
   - Operates with zero internet connection. Never freezes during provincial brownouts, rainy weather, or network blackouts.
2. **Goodbye Battered Cuaderno (Walang Kawalang Utang)**:
   - Digital, searchable credit ledger with itemized digital proof/SMS receipts and split-tender payment tracking.
3. **Kita Mo ang Tunay na Tubo (Bantay Sariling Gamit)**:
   - 2-tap tracking for personal family consumption (*sariling gamit*) at cost to prevent capital bleed. Real-time gross margin with privacy screen mask.
4. **Walang Monthly Subscription (Libre at Pangmatagalan)**:
   - Complete POS, inventory barcode scanning, and receipt printing with no hidden fees or locked features.
5. **Kalkulado ang Sukli at Barya**:
   - Built-in smart coin/bill calculator for odd payments (e.g., ₱64 for a ₱54 bill) to preserve scarce neighborhood change (*barya*).

## 🗓️ Acquisition Milestones & Growth Goals

- **Phase 1: Grassroots Pilot (Months 1–3)**:
  - 500 active sari-sari store installs in pilot cluster (e.g., Bulacan / Rizal / Quezon City barangays).
  - Target: 90-day retention $> 65\%$.
- **Phase 2: Wholesaler & Palengke Expansion (Months 4–6)**:
  - Direct tie-ins with Puregold, Super8, and Divisoria wholesale corridors via flyer distribution and QR posters.
  - Scale to 5,000 active merchant stores.
- **Phase 3: Viral Organic Loop (Months 7–12)**:
  - TikTok & Reels relatable skits highlighting *cuaderno* nightmares vs. digital empowerment.
  - Scale to 25,000+ stores nationwide.
