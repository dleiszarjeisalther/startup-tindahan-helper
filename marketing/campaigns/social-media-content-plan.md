# Social Media Content Strategy & Viral Video Scripts

In the Philippines, micro-entrepreneurs are deeply engaged on **TikTok** and **Facebook Groups** (e.g., *Sari-Sari Store Owners Philippines*, *Tindahan Negosyo Tips*, *Diskarte ng Pinoy Store*). Content that performs best combines:
1. **Relatable Everyday Humor & Frustration** (e.g., Suki insisting they paid, crumpled bills, wet coins).
2. **Practical Financial Literacy** (e.g., How *sariling gamit* secretly bankrupts stores).
3. **Product Demonstration as a Relief Solution** (how 1 tap fixes the problem).

## 🎬 Viral TikTok / Reels Short-Form Video Scripts

### Script 1: "Ang Misteryo ng Nawawalang Utang" (Relatable Drama / Comedy)
- **Duration**: 45–60 seconds
- **Tone**: Humorous, relatable, high energy
- **Characters**: Nanay Tindera, Suki (Kumare)

```text
[0:00 - 0:07] HOOK
Visual: Close-up of a tattered, coffee-stained spiral notebook with torn pages.
Nanay (frustrated, scratching head): "Jusko, saan na napunta yung listahan ni Mareng Gina?! Dito ko lang sinulat sa gilid ah!"
Sound Effect: Dramatic sound / screech.

[0:07 - 0:20] THE CONFLICT
Visual: Kumare arrives at the tindahan window with a ₱100 bill.
Kumare: "Mare, magkano utang ko nung Martes? ₱80 lang diba?"
Nanay flips through 10 crumpled pages: "Teka lang Mare... may kape, may suka, may chichirya... parang ₱240 ata?"
Kumare (frowning): "Bakit ₱240?! Baka pinatungan mo Mare ah!"
Caption on screen: "Kapag sa notebook nakalista, tampuhan ang abot."

[0:20 - 0:38] THE SOLUTION
Visual: Fast cut to Tindahan Helper app on a standard Android phone.
Nanay smiles, taps "Utang Ledger", types "Gina".
Nanay: "Aba, Mare! Gamit ko na 'tong Tindahan Helper! Eto o: Martes 10:15 AM: 1 Mantika ₱45, 2 Sardinas ₱50, Kabuuan ₱95!"
App sound: Satisfying chime.
Kumare inspects phone screen: "Ayy oo nga! Malinaw pa sa sikat ng araw! Pwede bang paki-send sa text?"
Nanay taps "Send Digital Receipt": "Sent agad, Mare!"

[0:38 - 0:50] CALL TO ACTION
Nanay looks at camera holding the phone:
"Mga ka-tindahan! Itapon na ang punit-punit na notebook! I-download ang Startup Tindahan Helper sa Play Store. Libre, offline, at walang monthly fee!"
On-screen text: "Link in bio / Search 'Startup Tindahan Helper' sa Google Play!"
```

### Script 2: "Bakit Walang Tubo ang Tindahan Mo?" (Educational & Reality Check)
- **Duration**: 45 seconds
- **Tone**: Thought-provoking, empathetic, business coaching
- **Focus**: Sariling Gamit (Family consumption)

```text
[0:00 - 0:06] HOOK
Visual: Store owner counting cash at midnight looking worried.
Voiceover (punchy): "Lakas ng benta mo buong araw... pero bakit pagdating ng gabi, walang naiwan sa kaha mo?"

[0:06 - 0:18] THE SILENT KILLER
Visual: Montages of family members taking items from shelves:
- Anak grabs a snack bag and soda without paying.
- Asawa grabs cooking oil and eggs for dinner.
Voiceover: "Ito ang tinatawag na 'Sariling Gamit.' Akala mo konti lang, pero sa isang buwan, ₱3,000 to ₱5,000 ng puhunan mo ang nauubos nang hindi mo namamalayan!"

[0:18 - 0:32] THE FIX
Visual: Owner opens Tindahan Helper.
Taps "Stock Adjustment" -> Selects "Sariling Gamit / Personal Use".
Voiceover: "Sa Startup Tindahan Helper, bawat kinuha ng pamilya, i-record sa puhunan cost sa loob lang ng 2 taps! Walang patong, pero kalkulado ang labas ng pera."

[0:32 - 0:45] CALL TO ACTION
Voiceover: "Protektahan ang puhunan mo para tuloy-tuloy ang asenso! 100% Offline, walang subscription. I-download na ngayon!"
```

### Script 3: "Si Ate Joy at ang Nakalilitong Sukli" (Speed & Accuracy)
- **Duration**: 30 seconds
- **Tone**: Fast-paced, comedic relief
- **Focus**: Odd change calculator & barya preservation

```text
[0:00 - 0:08] HOOK
Visual: Customer hands a ₱100 bill plus four ₱1 coins for a ₱54 bill.
Cashier helper looks terrified, eyes spinning in circles doing mental math.
Sound effect: Dial-up modem noise.

[0:08 - 0:18] THE HERO FEATURE
Visual: Ate Joy taps ₱104 in Tindahan Helper.
Screen instantly shows in giant green letters: "SUKLI: ₱50 (Isang Buong Papel)".
Ate Joy effortlessly hands back the clean ₱50 bill.

[0:18 - 0:30] CTA
Voiceover: "Wag nang magkamali sa sukli. Wag ubusin ang barya. Subukan ang Startup Tindahan Helper!"
```

## 📱 Facebook Group Seeding & Organic Strategy

### Key Target Communities
1. *Sari-Sari Store Owners Group Philippines* (500k+ members)
2. *Pinoy Negosyo & Tindahan Tips* (300k+ members)
3. *Micro-Retailers & Wholesalers PH* (150k+ members)

### Value-First Post Templates (No Spamming)
- **Format**: Share high-value templates or real-life store balance sheets.
  - *Sample Post*:
    > "Mga Ka-Tindahan, sino rito ang may problema sa pamilya na kumukuha ng paninda pang-ulam pero hindi naililista? Ganito ginawa ko para hindi malugi ang puhunan namin nang hindi nag-aaway sa bahay..."
    > *(Explains the method of recording at cost, shares screenshots of Tindahan Helper's reason-coded tracking, answers questions in comments).*
