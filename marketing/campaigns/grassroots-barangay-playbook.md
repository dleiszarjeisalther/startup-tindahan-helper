# Grassroots & Barangay Ground Activation Playbook

Sari-sari store owners rarely discover software through LinkedIn or corporate web ads. Their trusted information networks are:
1. **Wholesale Supply Hubs** (*Puregold, Super8, Divisoria, local public market palengke bodegas*).
2. **Barangay Hall Assemblies & Negosyo Centers** (*DTI / BMBE registrations*).
3. **Neighborhood Word-of-Mouth** (*Kapitbahay owners and suki recommendations*).

This playbook outlines hyper-local, field-proven execution strategies for on-the-ground store acquisition.

---

## 🏬 Activation Channel 1: The Wholesaler Corridors (Puregold & Super8 Lines)

### Target Environment
Sari-sari store owners spend 2–4 hours every week lined up at cashier lanes and loading bays in wholesale grocery stores (e.g., Puregold Tindahan ni Aling Puring lanes, Super8 Grocery Warehouse).

### Execution Strategy
1. **"Habang Nakapila" (While in Line) Flyers**:
   - Field brand ambassadors (*Kasosyo Promoters*) walk along the wholesale lines handing out palm-sized, cardstock flyers.
   - Core message: *"Pag-uwi mo sa tindahan mo, paano mo ililista ang mga binili mo ngayon? Wag nang manu-mano sa notebook!"*
2. **On-the-Spot 60-Second Install Station**:
   - Set up an outdoor mini-booth or roaming promoter near loading docks/tricycle terminals where owners pack their boxes.
   - Promoter offers a free cold beverage or durable canvas eco-bag (*Tindahan Helper Sako-Bag*) in exchange for downloading the app and scanning their first product barcode.

---

## 🏘️ Activation Channel 2: Barangay Negosyo Roadshow

### Target Environment
Monthly Barangay General Assemblies, Senior Citizen / Women’s Micro-enterprise Clubs, and DTI Barangay Negosyo Seminars.

### Execution Strategy
1. **"Anti-Utang Leakage" Workshop**:
   - Sponsor a 15-minute practical workshop titled: *"Paano Maiiwasan ang Luging ₱3,000–₱5,000 Buwan-buwan sa Nawawalang Listahan ng Utang"*.
   - Live demonstration showing how easily paper notebooks rip, get spilled on, or cause fights with neighbors vs. instant digital utang tracking with SMS receipt proof.
2. **Barangay Community Endorsement**:
   - Provide Barangay Kagawad on Livelihood / Committee on Trade with educational posters showing how digital record-keeping supports Barangay Micro-Business Enterprise (BMBE) legal compliance without tax headaches.

## 🏷️ Activation Channel 3: Point-of-Sale Counter Sticking Strategy

### "Dito, Malinaw ang Listahan!" Store Counter Badges
Provide enrolled store owners with high-visibility weather-resistant vinyl stickers to place right at their store grills or checkout window:

```
+-------------------------------------------------------+
|  [ ✓ ] DITO, MALINAW AT AUTOMATED ANG LISTAHAN!      |
|                                                       |
|  Bawat benta at utang, kalkulado at may resibo.       |
|  Powered by: Startup Tindahan Helper                  |
|  [ QR Code: I-download ang App nang Libre ]           |
+-------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Psychological Impact:
- **For the Suki**: Establishes that the store owner is fair, transparent, and accurate. Reduces disputes when credit statements are sent.
- **For Neighboring Store Owners**: Triggers curiosity and FOMO ("Anong app gamit ni Kumare? Bakit mas mabilis siya magsukli?").

---

## 🤝 Activation Channel 4: "Suki-to-Suki" Merchant Referral Mechanics

### Program Name: *"Kasosyo Referral Circle"*
- **The Mechanic**: Every existing store owner receives a unique 6-digit referral code.
- **The Incentive**:
  - When another store owner registers and enters the code:
    - Referrer receives a curated printable wholesale shopping guide or promo pack (e.g., free roll of thermal receipt paper or a pack of branded price tags).
    - Top 10 referring store owners each quarter receive a free Bluetooth ESC/POS 58mm Thermal Printer.
- **Zero Internet Requirement**: Referral codes can be entered offline and synced whenever an optional backup or brief network handshake occurs.
